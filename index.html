Questions, answered.

Do I need an account or a cloud workspace?

No. The engine runs on your Mac. Off your network, a relay forwards sealed frames between your phone and your Mac. With Tailscale Funnel or a quick tunnel there is no cloud account at all.

Which agents does it run?

Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Grok, Hermes, Kimi, Z.ai, Cursor, Gemini, Copilot and a local Ollama model. Each runs as it would in your terminal, on the subscription you already have.

Does it cost per token?

No. ctrl+all meters nothing. You pay the vendors you already pay, and nothing else.

Can my phone do something my Mac would refuse?

No. Permission modes are enforced on the Mac. A phone can lower its own rank for a session and can never raise it. Teammates get a role: owner, operator or viewer.

Is there a phone app?

A web client works on any phone today and installs to the Home Screen. A native iOS app is next.

What does the relay see?

Opaque bytes. Frames are sealed with X25519, Ed25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305 before they leave a device.
