Run your own relay.

The phone app reaches your Mac through a relay. Pro is the one we run for you. The free path is to run one yourself.

A relay is the ctrlall-relay process a phone connects through. Your Mac keeps one end-to-end-encrypted connection open to it, and a paired phone uses it to reach the Mac. The relay sees that your Mac is online and the size of each message. It cannot read a message, because frames are sealed on your devices.

A relay you run is yours: your machine or your account, your logs, no bill from us. Everything else is the same on either relay. The engine, the apps, every agent and every update stay free, and ctrlall relay show prints the relay in use.

Connect a relay you already run

The CLI ships inside the Mac app. Open Terminal and set a short name for this session:

alias ctrlall='/Applications/ctrl+all.app/Contents/MacOS/ctrlall'
ctrlall relay show

Use the public WebSocket address of your relay. Replace the example address below with your own address.

Finish active agent work first. A relay change can restart the engine and interrupt a live session.

ctrlall relay set wss://relay.example.com
ctrlall relay show

Pair your phone

Keep your Mac awake. Open the app's pairing screen, or run:

ctrlall pair

Scan the QR code with Android or iPhone. Approve the device on your Mac. Each teammate pairs a separate device.

Check the connection

A successful Mac health check proves only that the Mac can reach the relay. Your phone must reach it too.

A localhost address works only on the Mac. A LAN address needs the same network. A Tailscale address needs the same tailnet.

For access from another network, use a reachable public relay address. Keep the relay's phone client compatible with your Mac app.
